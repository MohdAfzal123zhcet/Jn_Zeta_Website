Privacy Policy

How the EviLock Android app handles your information.

Last updated: 13 November 2026 Developer: AoE Labs Package: com.evilock.android

About EviLock

EviLock is a self-control app. It blocks adult content, Shorts and Reels, limits social media use, and locks your phone during the hours you choose with Focus mode, Strict mode and Digital Curfew. It also shows your screen time, counts your steps and has a Habit tracker.

EviLock does not collect or store your personal data on any server, and we never sell it. Everything the app checks and saves stays on your own phone.

In short

  • No account, no login, no analytics.
  • Everything is checked on your phone.
  • We have no servers that receive your data.
  • We never sell your data.
  • Only Google Play (payments, updates) and Google AdMob (ads) are involved.

04When the app goes online

  • Time check: reads the correct time from public servers (google.com, cloudflare.com), so a lock can't be shortened by changing the phone's clock. No personal data is sent.
  • Payments: handled by Google Play Billing. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. We only learn whether a purchase succeeded.
  • Ads: Google AdMob shows ads in two places only: a full-screen ad on block pages after you tap the button there, and an optional video ad on the Premium page when you tap "Watch ad" to get free Premium time. Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. We do not receive this data.
  • App updates: Google Play checks whether a newer version of EviLock is available.

05Third parties

  • Google Play (payments and app updates): Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (ads): How Google uses data from apps that use its services

To limit ad tracking, open your phone's Settings → Google → Ads and delete or reset your advertising ID.

06Sharing and sale of data

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google Play and Google AdMob collect directly, as described in section 04.
  • The app has no analytics or tracking tools.

07This website

  • No tracking cookies, analytics or ads.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address.
  • The download counter adds one to a total. It stores no IP address or device ID.

08Keeping and deleting data

  • App data stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • Uninstalling EviLock deletes all of it. You can also use Settings → Apps → EviLock → Storage → Clear data.
  • While a lock you started is running, uninstalling is blocked until the lock ends.
  • We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.

09Children

  • EviLock is not made for children under 13.
  • A parent or guardian may install it on a phone they are responsible for.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10Security

Your data is never sent to our servers, so it can't be exposed in an online breach. On the phone it is protected by Android's app security.
