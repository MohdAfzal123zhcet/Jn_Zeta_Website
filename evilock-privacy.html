EviLock Privacy Policy

EviLock for Android, by AoE Labs. Last updated 13 November 2026.

In short

  • No account and no login.
  • Everything the app checks is checked on your phone.
  • We have no servers that receive your data.
  • We never sell or share your data.
  • The only third parties are Google Play (payments and app updates) and Google AdMob (ads on block pages).

1. Who we are

  • Developer: AoE Labs
  • App: EviLock (package com.evilock.android)
  • Contact: zetajn7@gmail.com

2. Data the app uses on your phone

All of this stays on your phone. None of it is uploaded or sent to us.

  • Screen content: text, web addresses and a temporary screenshot of the screen, checked by an AI model that runs on the phone, to find and block adult content, Shorts and Reels. Screenshots are checked and discarded at once. They are never saved.
  • App usage: which app is open and for how long, for screen time, daily limits, Focus mode, Strict mode and Digital Curfew.
  • Installed apps: so you can pick which apps to limit or block.
  • Habit tracker entries: the entries you log yourself.
  • Steps: your daily step count.
  • Height and weight (optional): entered by you, to estimate walking distance.
  • Your settings: locks, limits, schedules and blocked apps.
  • Diagnostic log: a small log of app events to fix problems. It leaves your phone only if you share it with us yourself.

3. Data we do not access

  • Contacts, messages and call logs
  • Location
  • Camera and microphone
  • Photos and files

4. Permissions and why we need them

  • Accessibility service: to detect and block adult content, Shorts and Reels, apply your app limits and locks, and stop EviLock from being removed during a lock you started. The app shows a clear notice and asks for your consent before you turn it on.
  • Device admin: only to stop EviLock from being uninstalled while a lock you started is running.
  • Usage access: to measure screen time and apply app limits.
  • Physical activity: to count steps and leave out steps while riding in a bus or car.
  • Notifications: for reminders (lock ended, Habit tracker, 9 AM and 10 PM greetings).
  • Close other apps: to close an app or browser right after it is blocked.
  • Run at start-up: to restart your active locks after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: only for the uses listed in section 5.

5. When the app goes online

  • Time check: reads the correct time from public servers (google.com, cloudflare.com), so a lock can't be shortened by changing the phone's clock. No personal data is sent.
  • Payments: handled by Google Play Billing. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. We only learn whether a purchase succeeded.
  • Ads: Google AdMob shows a full-screen ad on block pages, only after you tap a button there. Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. We do not receive this data.
  • App updates: Google Play checks whether a newer version of EviLock is available.

6. Third parties

  • Google Play (payments, app updates): Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (ads): How Google uses data from apps that use its services

To limit ad tracking, open your phone's Settings → Google → Ads and delete or reset your advertising ID.

7. Sharing and sale of data

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools in the app.

8. This website

  • No tracking cookies, analytics or ads.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address.
  • The download counter adds one to a total. It stores no IP address or device ID.

9. Keeping and deleting data

  • App data stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • Uninstalling EviLock deletes all of it. You can also use Settings → Apps → EviLock → Storage → Clear data.
  • While a lock you started is running, uninstalling is blocked until the lock ends.
  • We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.

10. Children

  • EviLock is not made for children under 13.
  • A parent or guardian may install it on a phone they are responsible for.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Security

Your data is never sent to our servers, so it can't be exposed in an online breach. On the phone it is protected by Android's app security.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
