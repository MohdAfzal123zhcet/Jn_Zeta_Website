Privacy Policy

EviLock is built to protect you, not to watch you. It works on your phone, not in the cloud. We have no servers that receive your data, no accounts and no tracking.

Who we are

Jn Zeta makes the EviLock app for Android and runs this website. For any privacy question, email zetajn7@gmail.com.

What the app uses on your phone, and why

  • Accessibility: to read text on screen in browsers and YouTube, and to check video screens, so adult content can be blocked. It also stops the app from being switched off while an Iron Lock is running.
  • Videos on your phone: to check saved videos for adult content.
  • Usage access: to measure screen time, apply your daily limits and Digital Curfew, and show your reports.
  • Device admin: only to stop EviLock from being uninstalled while an Iron Lock is running.
  • Physical activity: to count your daily steps.
  • Notifications and running in the background: so protection keeps working and restarts after your phone reboots.

What stays on your phone

Everything the app checks is processed on your device. Screen text, screenshots of video screens and saved videos are analysed on the phone and are never saved, uploaded or sent to us. Your screen time, limits, streaks, steps, height and weight are stored only in the app on your phone.

The app keeps a small diagnostic log on your phone to help fix problems. It leaves your phone only if you choose to share it with us yourself.

When the app goes online

  • Time check: to read the correct time from public servers so the Iron Lock can't be shortened by changing the phone's clock. No personal data is sent.
  • Payments: handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank details. Razorpay processes them under its own privacy policy. We only receive the payment status and payment ID.

Download counter

We show how many times EviLock has been downloaded. When you tap a download button, your browser keeps a small hidden note so the same browser is counted only once, and our server only adds one to a total. We do not store your IP address, a device ID or anything that identifies you.

This website

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics or ads. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address as part of delivering them.

Children

Parents and guardians may install EviLock on a phone they are responsible for, such as their child's. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

All app data lives on your phone. Uninstalling EviLock (once no Iron Lock is running) removes it. There is nothing stored with us to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
